# Brand & Positioning — BytePets

> Living doc. How we talk about the product and how we price it. Changing this doc is a council-level change (see `AGENTS.md` → "Editing rules for living docs").

## One-line pitch
The Vilnius pet map that owners keep true: an iPhone app to find, add, check and share pet places and lost or found pets, ask an AI assistant about your dog, and find walking buddies.

## Positioning
For pet owners in Vilnius who today search Facebook groups, forums and old websites for help. BytePets puts the city's pet places, a smart assistant, and other owners to walk with in one place. Owners add places and confirm that they are still correct, so every place shows where it came from and when it was last checked. Unlike general pet apps, it is local: it knows Vilnius parks, vets and walking areas. Lost and found pets sit on the same map, and anyone can add a sighting. The map and lost & found serve every pet; the assistant and walks start with dogs.

## Voice & tone
- Warm, friendly and calm. We talk like a helpful neighbour who also has a dog.
- Short, clear sentences.
- Do: be honest about what the AI knows. Say "see a vet" when it matters.
- Don't: give a diagnosis, scare people, or promise that a place is open without a source.

## Key messages
1. One pet map of Vilnius, checked by the owners who use it. Every place shows its source and when it was last confirmed.
2. Lost or found a pet? Post it on the map in a minute. Neighbours add sightings with one tap.
3. Ask anything about your dog, with a photo, and get a clear answer and the nearest place that can help.
4. Never walk alone — find dog owners near you and walk together.
