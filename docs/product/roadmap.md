# Roadmap — BytePets

> Living doc. The current plan of what ships next and why. Changing this doc is a council-level change (see `AGENTS.md` → "Editing rules for living docs"). Use **Phase 1 / Phase 2 / Phase 3** naming, not V1 / V2 / V3.

## Now (in progress)
**Phase 1 — Hack4Vilnius MVP (24 hours).** iPhone app + landing page: a pet map that owners check, add to and share; lost & found posts with sightings on the same map; AI assistant with photos and map hand-off; Walk-Mate wall, create and join (3D preview if time allows); anonymous sign-in and dog profile. Every item the challenge brief names is built. Approved in `decisions/council/001-hackathon-mvp-scope.md`, `decisions/council/002-match-mvp-to-challenge-brief.md` and `decisions/council/003-build-every-item-in-the-brief.md`. Build plan: `hackathon-plan.md`.

## Next
**Phase 2 — after the hackathon (if we continue).**
- Sign in with Apple and account recovery, so each person's check counts once and lost & found posts have a real owner
- Full 3D walk preview: all scene types and the live walk-in
- Alerts for lost pets near you (push notifications)
- Google Search grounding with sources in the assistant
- Push notifications for walk joins and reminders
- Leave, edit and cancel walks; share a walk link
- Google Maps grounding with pins for any place
- Show the exact start point only to people who joined (privacy)

## Later
- AI photo matching between lost and found posts
- A web page for each shared place and post
- A reputation system and weighted votes; editing a place's details; moderation of owner-added places
- More kinds of pets
- Free pin drop for walks with scene lookup; forecast weather in the 3D scene
- Android app (Flutter makes this cheaper)
- A business model (ideas: premium assistant, featured listings for vets and pet shops)
