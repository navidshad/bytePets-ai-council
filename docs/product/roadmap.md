# Roadmap — BytePets

> Living doc. The current plan of what ships next and why. Changing this doc is a council-level change (see `AGENTS.md` → "Editing rules for living docs"). Use **Phase 1 / Phase 2 / Phase 3** naming, not V1 / V2 / V3.

## Now (in progress)
**Phase 1 — Hack4Vilnius MVP (24 hours).** iPhone app + landing page: Walk-Mate with 3D preview, AI assistant with photos and map hand-off, dog-services map, anonymous sign-in and dog profile. Approved in `decisions/council/001-hackathon-mvp-scope.md`. Build plan: `hackathon-plan.md`.

## Next
**Phase 2 — after the hackathon (if we continue).**
- Sign in with Apple and account recovery
- Push notifications for walk joins and reminders
- Leave, edit and cancel walks; share a walk link
- Google Maps grounding with pins for any place
- Show the exact start point only to people who joined (privacy)

## Later
- **AI lost & found** (photo matching, alerts by area) — on hold
- Crowdsourced checks of places and a reputation system
- Android app (Flutter makes this cheaper)
- A business model (ideas: premium assistant, featured listings for vets and pet shops)
