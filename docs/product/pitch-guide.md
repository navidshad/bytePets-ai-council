# Pitch Guide — Hack4Vilnius 2026

> From the organisers' pitch-guide slides and our notes at the event. Build plan: `hackathon-plan.md`.

| Lunch | The jury reviews the videos. |

Submit at least an hour early; you can edit until the cutoff. If the internet or hardware fails, tell the organisers (Ignas) at once, and they take it by USB or on their PC.

## Format on stage
- **~3 min** talk: team, challenge, problem with data, solution, users, city benefit, next steps.
- **≥ 2 min** live demo of the working prototype. Bring the phone or laptop up if it can't sit in the slides.
- **3 min** jury questions.
- English or Lithuanian. **One main speaker**; the rest of the team on stage for the demo and questions.

## Slides
- Formats: PPT, PPTX or PDF; Canva, Gamma, Google Slides or Prezi only as a public link. **A backup PDF is required.**
- Put it in our team folder on the shared Google Drive, and paste the link on VeloxEntry (Hackathon → Submission).
- **Check that every link opens in an incognito window without signing in.**
- Good slides: one idea per slide, large font, screenshots instead of descriptions, team name and challenge on slide 1.

## How the jury judges
Same criteria in the pre-pitch and the final:
1. **Innovation and benefit to Vilnius** — what is new, what improves, by how much.
2. **Business and market potential** — who uses it, who pays, how it grows.
3. **Execution and use of open data** — what really works, which open data.
4. **Pitch quality** — a clear story, on time, confident answers.

**Jury:** Gintarė Galinaitė (Cognizant Technology Solutions Lithuania), Irmantas Znatinas (Vilnius City Municipality), Lina Adomonienė (Ignitis Group). Prepare for three views: **technology, the city and business.**

## Common mistakes to avoid
Intro too long · listing the tech stack · demo won't load (no backup recording) · too much text · over time (the ending gets cut) · unclear user.

## Our pitch (5 min)
| # | Part | Time | What we say or show |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Team | 15 s | Names and roles on one slide. One sentence on why us. |
| 2 | Challenge and problem | 45 s | Challenge name. A person: *"Rūta, 31, from Žvėrynas. Her dog Luna ran off at Vingis Park. She posted in three Facebook groups. Someone posted a found dog in a fourth group. The two posts never met."* Plus: "Is this café dog-friendly?" posts with no answer. |
| 3 | Open data | — | **Dataset:** city walking areas (35) and the pet register (55,384 dogs). **Numbers (straight from the source, no calculations):** 55,384 registered dogs; 35 city walking areas (4 being built, 8 planned); 165 pets reported lost in Vilnius in 2026 so far, 68 in all of 2025. **In the product:** every area is on the map with a badge, and walks and confirms keep it fresh. |
| 3 | Solution | 60 s | *"We help pet owners in Vilnius find info they can trust and find lost pets, by letting the community add, confirm and share it on one map, with an assistant that does it for you."* Two or three app screenshots. |
| 4 | Demo | 2 min | One story from start to finish (below). |
| 5 | Users | 20 s | 55,384 dog owners and ~102,000 pet owners in Vilnius; people who find an animal. Who pays: not the users (see Q&A). |
| 6 | Benefit to the city | 20 s | Pets reunited faster; one place for city pet data. **One indicator:** places confirmed by owners in the last 30 days. |
| 7 | Next steps | 20 s | A pilot with the city's walking-area data and 2 shelters. What we need: a monthly city data feed and a shelter partner. |

**Demo story (2 min, one scenario):** On Home, type to the assistant: *"I found a small grey dog near Vingis Park"* + photo → it shows a draft → Confirm → "Possible match: Luna, 1.2 km away" appears on both phones → both tap Yes → a private chat opens. Then open Vingis Park: "Official · city data", tonight's walk in 3D, tap Join and the avatar walks in. End on a café: a judge's phone taps "Still true", and the badge turns green.

Prepare all demo data in advance: signed in, posts seeded, no waiting. Say honestly what is seeded. Record a backup video of the same story.

## Jury questions — our answers
Answer first, then explain. Agree beforehand who takes tech and who takes business.

| Question | Our answer |
|---|---|
| How is it different from what exists? | Facebook groups and Google don't show if info is still true, and lost and found posts never meet. We show who confirmed what and when, and we match lost with found. |
| Where will the data come from long term? | City and VMVT open data, re-imported each month, plus owners confirming. Next: vets and shelters keep their own listings. |
| Who will pay? | Owners use the map free. People register walk slots, owners book them, and we take a small fee (we'll test 15%). 56 Vilnius requests for walking and care on one site, most never answered, show the demand. Backups: insurance quotes from a partner insurer, and sponsored listings for pet stores. |
| What really worked, what was a mock-up? | Real: imported city and VMVT data, add and confirm, matching, walks, the assistant. Seeded: demo posts, walks and a few confirms. |
| First step next month? | A pilot in one district with the city's walking-area data and two shelters, measuring places confirmed and pets reunited. |
| Benefit to Vilnius? | Pets get home faster, owners find trusted vets and places, and the city gets a live pet map. Measured by places confirmed in the last 30 days. |
