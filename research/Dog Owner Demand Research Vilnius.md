# Dog Owner Demand Research: Vilnius

Oct 9, 2026 · @Stas

## Summary

The evidence strongly supports paid dog walking and pet sitting, including in Vilnius. It only weakly supports group walks with other owners: owners clearly meet each other on walks, but no source shows they would use a service to arrange it.

| Claim | Global evidence | Vilnius evidence | Strength |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Owners want group walks with other owners | Brand-commissioned surveys (Purina, Rover, PetSafe) and one peer-reviewed study | None found; nearest is a request for outdoor group puppy classes | Weak |
| Owners look for good places to walk | Sniffspot bookings; surveys on dog-friendly venues | 21 dog parks in the city; rules restrict dogs to park edges and designated areas | Medium |
| Owners pay someone to walk their dog | IBISWorld, Rover SEC filings, APPA | Walking requests on paslaugos.lt | Strong |
| Owners need care while away | Rover, APPA; Polish surveys | 56 care requests on paslaugos.lt; Boop's 15,000 monthly members | Strong |

Facebook groups and Reddit could not be read for this report (login wall and blocked access). The gaps section lists how to collect that data.

## Claim 1: owners want to meet other owners on walks

Owners do meet each other on walks, which is the opening for group walks. But meeting people is rarely why they go out, so a group-walk feature needs testing before it is built.

&#91;embedded content: Rover trend report; Purina UK poll (Apr 2024); Mars Petcare/HABRI; Wood et al. via Psychology Today; dog park study via K9 of Mine\]

- **Dog walking drives local ties.** In the peer-reviewed study, dog owners were five times more likely than other pet owners to get to know people in their community. The effect came from owners who walk their dog regularly.
- **Friendships rarely go further.** A study of four Oklahoma City dog parks found visitors called them friendly, but did not report friendships continuing outside the park.
- **Safety is a second motive.** The founder of the UK group-walk app MorePaws built it after his mother felt nervous walking her puppy alone.
- **A nearby signal, not from Vilnius.** A pitch deck for a Polish dog-walk matching app reports that most surveyed owners in one Warsaw district want more contact with other dogs; only 4% said their dog's contact was enough.

## Claim 2: owners look for good places to walk

Owners travel and pay for better places to walk their dogs. In Vilnius, rules that keep dogs to park edges and designated areas make knowing where to go genuinely useful.

**Global evidence**

- **People pay for private space.** Sniffspot, which rents out yards as private dog parks, has had over 20,000 hosts sign up in 3,600 US cities and averages about 25,000 bookings a month ([Kinship](https://preview.dev.kinship.com/dog-lifestyle/sniffspot)).
- **Even yard owners use it.** 70% of Sniffspot guests have their own yard ([GeekWire](https://go.upcontent.com/b936e5c7-8a36-4a9f-a957-d0fc8083ac13)).
- **Owners travel for a better park.** In PetSafe's survey of 2,000 owners, 25% travel further, up to 18 miles on average, to reach the park they prefer. Also, 66% go to a park where their dog has more friends ([PetSafe](https://www.petsafe.com/blog/pet-parent-survey-the-perfect-park/)).
- **Dog-friendly venues are hard to find.** In a DogBuddy survey of 2,500+ UK owners, a third struggle to find a dog-friendly pub ([DogBuddy](https://www.goodreads.com/author_blog_posts/13873970-brits-shun-pubs-partners-that-aren-t-dog-friendly)). In a Kennel Club survey, 40% had trouble finding a restaurant that welcomes their dog ([VetSurgeon](https://www.vetsurgeon.org/b/veterinary-news/posts/69940)).

**Vilnius**

- **A third of the country's dog parks.** Vilnius city municipality has 21 of Lithuania's 63 dog parks ([POIdata](https://www.poidata.io/report/dog-park/lithuania)).
- **Known spots:** Vingis Park, the Purina Dog Park in Šnipiškės, an enclosure by the Neris in Žirmūnai, and a dog beach in Vingis ([International House Vilnius](https://ihvilnius.lt/guide-to-lithuania-exploring-vilnius-with-your)).
- **The city is still adding space.** A planned square in Viršuliškės includes a dog-walking zone ([kaunieciams.lt](https://kaunieciams.lt/vietoj-pelkynes-naujas-skveras-virsuliskese)).
- **Rules restrict where dogs can go.** Lithuanian rules ban dogs from parks, squares and lawns except at the edges or in designated areas ([Finonamai](https://finonamai.lt/informacija/butai-kuriuose-neleidziama-laikyti-sunu/)). That summary quotes fines in litas, so it is old; check the current Vilnius rules (in force since 1 October 2023) before relying on it.

## Claim 3: owners pay someone to walk their dog

Paid dog walking is a proven, growing and fragmented market. The main barrier is trust, not demand.

| Measure | Figure | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| US dog-walking market, 2026 | $1.3bn; 8.9% a year growth 2020–2025 | [IBISWorld](https://www.ibisworld.com/united-states/industry/dog-walking-services/4350/) |
| Market concentration | No company holds more than 5% | [IBISWorld](https://www.ibisworld.com/united-states/industry/dog-walking-services/4350/) |
| Rover platform, to Sept 2023 | 93m+ services booked by 4m+ pet parents; 1m+ providers paid | [SEC 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1826018/000182601823000064/ex991_11292023.htm) |
| Rover bookings value, Jan–Sept 2023 | $742m, up 28% year on year | [SEC 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1826018/000182601823000058/rovr-20230930.htm) |
| Rover sale to Blackstone | About $2.3bn | [Blackstone](https://www.blackstone.com/news/press/rover-agrees-to-be-acquired-by-blackstone-in-2-3-billion-transaction/) |
| US owners using apps like Rover or Wag | Up from 13% to 17% in two years | [APPA via Global Pet Industry](https://globalpetindustry.com/article/us-pet-ownership-returns-to-pre-pandemic-levels) |

**The underlying need**

- **Dogs left alone.** In the US, 40% of dogs stay home alone while owners work, and 12% rely on friends or family ([APPA](https://globalpetindustry.com/article/us-pet-ownership-returns-to-pre-pandemic-levels)). In the UK, 19% of dogs are left alone for five or more hours on a typical day ([PDSA PAW 2024](https://www.pdsa.org.uk/what-we-do/pdsa-animal-wellbeing-report/paw-report-2024/dogs)).
- **Work gets cut short.** 47% of employed US dog owners often leave work early to let their dog out ([Rover](https://www.rover.com/blog/rover-trend-report/)).

**The barrier: trust**

- 83% of owners have worried that their walker cuts corners ([Rover](https://www.rover.com/blog/rover-trend-report/)).
- In user interviews, owners hesitate to pay when friends or family can help, and find it hard to trust strangers ([case study](https://dribbble.com/shots/20314011-Case-Study-Woof-n-it-a-dog-walking-app)).

## Claim 4: owners need care for their pets while away

Finding care while away is a real pain: many owners skip trips over it. In the region, though, few pay a professional yet; most rely on family.

- **US owners skip travel.** 56% of US dog owners have skipped travel because of the cost of pet care, rising to 64% of millennial owners ([Rover](https://www.rover.com/blog/rover-trend-report/)).
- **Owners worry while away.** 82% of dog owners worry about their dog when they're away ([Rover](https://www.rover.com/blog/rover-trend-report/)).
- **Polish owners give up holidays.** Each year 30% of Polish pet owners give up a holiday because they have no one to leave their pet with (Petsy / Zymetria, August 2024, via [Wakacje.pl](https://www.wakacje.pl/p/najlepsiewakacje.html)).
- **Paid care is still rare in Poland.** Only 7% of Polish owners use professional care during holidays; most rely on family or friends, and most spend at most about 200 zł on it (BIG InfoMonitor, n=1,049, June 2024, via [Bank.pl](https://bank.pl/?p=486377)).
- **Owners prefer care at home.** In a FETCH! Pet Care survey, 82% chose in-home sitting as the best kind of care; kennels got 0.27% ([Franchising.com](https://www.franchising.com/amp/news/20071030_survey_reveals_majority_of_pet_owners_consider_ken.html)). That survey dates from 2007.

The Polish figures are the closest available proxy for Lithuania. They suggest room to grow, but you would have to win over owners who currently use family for free. Vilnius request data follows in the next section.

## Vilnius: what local owners are asking for

Vilnius owners post regular, real requests for sitting and walking, and some get filled. Budgets are low, and demand clusters around the summer holidays.

**Other local signals**

- **Prices are low.** The Vilnius care category lists prices of €2–15, averaging €8 ([paslaugos.lt](https://paslaugos.lt/sunu-viesbuciai/vilniuje)).
- **Owners want group sessions outdoors.** One owner asked for a beginner puppy group class in central Vilnius, group-only for socialisation, ideally outdoors in a park ([paslaugos.lt](https://paslaugos.lt/uzklausos/sunu-dresuotojai/sunu-dresavimas/vilniuje)). This is the nearest local evidence for group walks.
- **A funded local competitor.** Boop, a Vilnius pet-sitting app founded in 2021, reported about 15,000 active members a month and 30,000+ community members in Lithuania. It raised €300,000 at a €3m valuation in November 2023 and planned to expand to Poland and Finland ([AIN](https://en.ain.ua/2023/11/22/boop-secures-300k-reaches-3m-in-valuation)).
- **Market size.** No Vilnius-only dog count was found. The national register lists 305,600 dogs, and other estimates put dogs and cats at about 1.2 million because many go unregistered ([Finonamai](https://finonamai.lt/informacija/suns-laikymas-bute/)). The Agricultural Data Centre (ŽŪDC) can be asked for the count by municipality.
- **Expat demand (older).** A US diplomat in Vilnius wrote in 2017 that an expat-run dog-walking service would be welcome ([Tales Mag](https://www.talesmag.com/real-post-report/lithuania/vilnius/2294)).

## Gaps and next steps

The biggest gap is Vilnius-specific evidence for group walks; Facebook groups and a short survey are the quickest way to fill it.

- **Facebook and Reddit were not reachable.** Facebook groups need a login, and Reddit blocked access from the research tool.
- **The request sample is small.** The chart covers the 14 newest of 56 requests, from one marketplace.
- **Many global figures come from brand surveys.** Purina, Rover and PetSafe commissioned the surveys behind most social and park figures.

* [ ] Search Facebook for "Šunys Vilniuje", "Šunų vedžiojimas Vilnius", "Petsitter Vilnius" and "Expats in Vilnius"; note member counts and posts asking for sitters, walkers or walking buddies
* [ ] Search r/vilnius and r/lithuania for "dog", "sitter" and "walk"
* [ ] Ask the Agricultural Data Centre (ŽŪDC) for registered dogs in Vilnius city municipality
* [ ] Run a short survey in the Facebook groups: would owners join an organised group walk, how often, and would they pay
* [ ] Page through all 56 paslaugos.lt requests to confirm the July peak
