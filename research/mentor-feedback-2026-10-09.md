# Mentor feedback — 2026-10-09

> One mentor session at Hack4Vilnius. The mentor owns two dogs and a cat in Vilnius and uses Boop (a pet-sitting app). One person's experience: a strong signal, not data.

## What they said

**Walking and walk slots**
- Flexibility matters: owners want to agree or say no per walk, not commit for long.
- Proof of the walk builds trust. One Boop walker sent photos and short updates; another sent nothing, and the owner (abroad) worried the dog had run away. Making a simple check-in or photo the default would make owners happier. It must stay easy for the walker.
- Owners differ: some want only "done", some want photos, some want a live video or live GPS tracking.
- Finding the right walker: owners need to know what dogs a walker can handle (big dogs, reactive dogs, only cats, only small dogs). Boop has only free text, no tags.
- Location: Boop shows only the rough neighbourhood, never the address, and sorts walkers by distance. Good model.
- Boop's best feature: a free first meeting (about 30 minutes) before booking.
- Boop's worst: slow messages (minutes to arrive). Fast chat would stand out.
- Price is agreed per job (two dogs plus feeding a cat costs more).
- "We need to figure out the business mechanism" — still the biggest open question.

**Places and walking areas**
- Owners walk near home, in fields and forests, not 1–2 km away to a city walking area.
- Pet-friendly cafés: visited "very rarely".
- Owners of unfriendly or reactive dogs need quiet places and times, with no other dogs or cats (like Google Maps' "how busy" times).
- Group walks: friendly dogs meet anyway; low value.

**Vets and emergencies — the strongest need**
- The real problem: which vet is **open now** (nights, weekends, holidays). Most close by 21:00; very few work late.
- Real case: a cat was hurt late in the evening; the nearest clinic was closing and sent them elsewhere. Correct, current hours would have saved time.
- Hours change (holidays), so they must be checked. Ideas: call every clinic every ~2 weeks (Vilnius has about 100 places at most), or connect to clinic booking systems. Clinics will join to be visible, but won't spend time on it.
- Owners also need to know which animals a clinic treats (some don't take rabbits) and any specialists (e.g. a cat specialist).

**Not worth building**
- Step or activity tracking for dogs: owners don't care, and devices already do it.
