# Challenger feedback — 2026-10-09

> A talk with the person from If Insurance who proposed Challenge #6. This shows what the challenge was meant to solve, so it outweighs other feedback. One person's view, not data.

## What they said

**The two most valuable features: dog playgrounds (walking areas) and dog-friendly places.**
- **Playgrounds:** apps show them on a map, but you can't tell if one is any good. Some are old, not renovated, or have a broken fence. People who went there should be able to **rate it**, add **photos**, and say what **equipment** it has and whether it suits **big or small dogs**. Then you can show only well-rated ones, or filter by equipment and dog size.
- **Dog-friendly cafés and restaurants:** many don't welcome dogs or have nothing for them (no dog menu or treats). Owners want to find the ones that do.
- **Use case:** "I want to explore a different part of the city: stop for a coffee where my dog is welcome, then go to a playground I've seen in photos, with the equipment I want."

**One app for everything.** They prefer more in one place: vets, clinics, pet shops, playgrounds, cafés. "Then I could forget about Facebook groups and Google Maps."

**Insurance view.**
- Most pet insurance claims are **vet clinic invoices**; it depends a lot on the breed.
- Pet insurance has become much more popular in recent years.
- People buy it so their pet gets proper care: they go to a vet instead of googling or waiting.
- They had no exact numbers; suggested looking for articles online.

**Not a top feature:** logging how long a dog is left alone or walked. Only useful in narrow cases (dog hotels and sitters track this themselves).
