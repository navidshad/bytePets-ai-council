# Feedback — Hack4Vilnius, 2026-10-09

Two talks during the hackathon. The challenger's view outweighs the mentor's, because they wrote Challenge #6 and said what it is meant to solve. Each is one person's view, not data.

1. **Challenger** — the person from If Insurance who proposed Challenge #6.
2. **Mentor** — a Vilnius owner of two dogs and a cat who uses Boop (a pet-sitting app).

## 1. Challenger (If Insurance)

**The two most valuable features: dog playgrounds (walking areas) and dog-friendly places.**
- **Playgrounds:** apps show them on a map, but you can't tell if one is any good. Some are old, not renovated, or have a broken fence. People who went there should be able to **rate it**, add **photos**, and say what **equipment** it has and whether it suits **big or small dogs**. Then you can show only well-rated ones, or filter by equipment and dog size.
- **Dog-friendly cafés and restaurants:** many don't welcome dogs or have nothing for them (no dog menu or treats). Owners want to find the ones that do.
- **Use case:** "I want to explore a different part of the city: stop for a coffee where my dog is welcome, then go to a playground I've seen in photos, with the equipment I want."

**One app for everything.** They prefer more in one place: vets, clinics, pet shops, playgrounds, cafés. "Then I could forget about Facebook groups and Google Maps."

**Insurance view.**
- Most pet insurance claims are **vet clinic invoices**; it depends a lot on the breed.
- Pet insurance has become much more popular in recent years.
- People buy it so their pet gets proper care: they go to a vet instead of googling or waiting.
- They had no exact numbers; suggested looking for articles online.

**Not a top feature:** logging how long a dog is left alone or walked. Only useful in narrow cases (dog hotels and sitters track this themselves).

## 2. Mentor (dog owner, Boop user)

**Walking and walk slots**
- Flexibility matters: owners want to agree or say no per walk, not commit for long.
- Proof of the walk builds trust. One Boop walker sent photos and short updates; another sent nothing, and the owner (abroad) worried the dog had run away. Making a simple check-in or photo the default would make owners happier. It must stay easy for the walker.
- Owners differ: some want only "done", some want photos, some want a live video or live GPS tracking.
- Finding the right walker: owners need to know what dogs a walker can handle (big dogs, reactive dogs, only cats, only small dogs). Boop has only free text, no tags.
- Location: Boop shows only the rough neighbourhood, never the address, and sorts walkers by distance. Good model.
- Boop's best feature: a free first meeting (about 30 minutes) before booking.
- Boop's worst: slow messages (minutes to arrive). Fast chat would stand out.
- Price is agreed per job (two dogs plus feeding a cat costs more).
- "We need to figure out the business mechanism" — still the biggest open question.

**Places and walking areas**
- Owners walk near home, in fields and forests, not 1–2 km away to a city walking area.
- Pet-friendly cafés: visited "very rarely".
- Owners of unfriendly or reactive dogs need quiet places and times, with no other dogs or cats (like Google Maps' "how busy" times).
- Group walks: friendly dogs meet anyway; low value.

**Vets and emergencies — the strongest need**
- The real problem: which vet is **open now** (nights, weekends, holidays). Most close by 21:00; very few work late.
- Real case: a cat was hurt late in the evening; the nearest clinic was closing and sent them elsewhere. Correct, current hours would have saved time.
- Hours change (holidays), so they must be checked. Ideas: call every clinic every ~2 weeks (Vilnius has about 100 places at most), or connect to clinic booking systems. Clinics will join to be visible, but won't spend time on it.
- Owners also need to know which animals a clinic treats (some don't take rabbits) and any specialists (e.g. a cat specialist).

**Not worth building**
- Step or activity tracking for dogs: owners don't care, and devices already do it.

## Where they agree and differ

| Topic | Challenger | Mentor |
|---|---|---|
| Dog playgrounds / walking areas | Top need: ratings, photos, equipment, big/small fit, condition | Walks near home, in fields and forests |
| Dog-friendly cafés | Top need | Goes very rarely |
| One app for everything | Yes | — |
| Vets open now | Most insurance claims are vet invoices | Strongest need: checked hours, nights and holidays |
| Logging dog care | Not a top feature | Step tracking not worth building |
| Group walks | — | Low value |
| Walk slots (paid walking) | — | Needs proof of walk, walker tags, rough location, free first meeting, fast chat |

They describe two moments: the **daily walk near home** (mentor: open vet, trusted walker) and a **trip to another part of the city** (challenger: a good playground, a dog-friendly café).
